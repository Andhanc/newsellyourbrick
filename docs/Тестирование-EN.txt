SellYourBrick Testing

Short scenario for 10 people: 6 buyers and 4 sellers. Goal — complete 3 deals on the site.
Required deals: B1 buys LOT-A from S1 via Buy Now; B2 buys debt LOT-B from S2 via Buy Now; B5 buys a share of LOT-C from S3.
Extra: B4 plays auction LOT-D (bids only); B6 books test-drive LOT-E; B3 opens VIP and LOT-F.
Rules: use only the test card; never tap pay twice; only assigned people may buy LOT-A/B/C.
IMPORTANT: auctions have not ended yet, so a buyer cannot win a listing through auction. In auction mode you can only play (bids, deposit, KYC, notifications). Real purchases must go only through Buy Now or share purchase.
1. Test card for all payments

Use this Stripe Test card for any payment on the site:
1. Card number: 4242 4242 4242 4242
2. Expiry: any future date, e.g. 12/34
3. CVC: any 3 digits, e.g. 123
4. Name on card: anything, e.g. Test User
5. Country / ZIP: anything, e.g. Spain / 28001
Use this card for: subscriptions, listing publication, deposit, Buy Now reserve, shares, test-drive.
2. Notification checks: email and WhatsApp

After every important action, check 3 places:
1. In-app notifications in the cabinet.
2. Email inbox of the account.
3. WhatsApp message if a phone number is linked / WhatsApp notifications are enabled.
Must check notifications for:
1. Registration / email confirmation.
2. Payment of subscription, deposit, reserve, share purchase, test-drive.
3. New auction bid / outbid.
4. Test-drive request to seller; approve / reject to buyer.
5. Buy Now deal completion for buyer and seller.
If email or WhatsApp did not arrive — screenshot the cabinet and tell the coordinator. Do not fail the deal only for that if the site status is correct.
3. Coordinator tasks

1. Assign roles, emails, language, test card, and lot names.
2. Approve seller listings in admin.
3. Confirm buyer KYC.
4. After LOT-A and LOT-B reserve payment — complete the purchase request in admin.
5. If needed, enable VIP access / mark LOT-F as a private lot.
6. Confirm WhatsApp test notifications are enabled on the staging environment.
Important: a paid reserve is not the final sale yet. The coordinator sets the final status.
4. Shared buyer start

1. Open the site → choose your language → refresh.
2. On the home page review 4 strategies: Auction, Buy Now, Debts, Shares.
3. Register as a buyer and confirm email → check the email.
4. Fill in the profile and save. If there is a phone/WhatsApp field — enter the test number.
5. Open Subscriptions and continue with your plan below.
5. Buyers — step-by-step paths

B1 — Starter: compared and bought LOT-A
1. Stay on free Starter.
2. Go to Trading → Buy Now.
3. Find LOT-A and 2 similar listings → add to favorites.
4. Open Favorites → Compare → choose LOT-A.
5. Tap Buy Now → open PDF → consent → signature.
6. Check 10% reserve → cancel payment once → confirm it is not purchased.
7. Pay with card 4242… → find the reserve in history → send the ID to the coordinator.
8. Check email and WhatsApp about the reserve / purchase.
9. Wait for coordinator completion → check site, email, WhatsApp again → reconcile with S1.
Done: LOT-A bought from S1 via Buy Now; notifications checked.
B2 — Pro: calculated in Smart Panel and bought debt LOT-B
1. Buy Pro with card 4242…: cancel once, then pay → confirm activation.
2. Check email/WhatsApp about subscription activation.
3. Go to Trading → Debts → find LOT-B → open risk info → add to favorites.
4. Open Smart Panel → calculate a regular listing and LOT-B → choose LOT-B.
5. Buy Now → PDF → consent → signature → pay reserve with card 4242…
6. Check email/WhatsApp about reserve → send ID to coordinator → wait for completion → reconcile with S2.
7. Extra: in Smart Panel on LOT-D check the lock on the share strategy.
Done: debt LOT-B bought from S2 via Buy Now; notifications checked.
B3 — VIP: opened Private Club and LOT-F
1. Without VIP try club, manager, and LOT-F — should be blocked.
2. Buy VIP with card 4242… or enter a promo code (wrong first, then correct).
3. Check email/WhatsApp about VIP activation.
4. Open VIP Club → LOT-F → documents → location → personal manager.
5. Log out → confirm guest cannot open LOT-F → log in again.
6. If coordinator allows, buy LOT-F via Buy Now with card 4242… and check notifications.
Done: VIP unlocks private content; notifications checked.
B4 — Starter: deposit, KYC, play auction LOT-D
1. Open LOT-D → place bid without deposit — should block.
2. Wallet → top up deposit to 3000 EUR or more with card 4242…
3. Check email/WhatsApp about deposit top-up.
4. Complete KYC → wait for coordinator confirmation.
5. Bid below step — error; then place a valid bid.
6. Ask another participant to outbid → check site, email, and WhatsApp notifications.
7. Place one more bid.
Note: until the auction timer ends you cannot win and buy via auction. Do not wait for a win — buy listings only via Buy Now in other scenarios. Here it is enough to check bids and notifications.
Done: at least two competing bids; auction purchase is not required in this test.

Done: LOT-C share bought once; notifications checked.

Done: test-drive completed; confirm/reject notifications checked.

3. Fill in seller profile and save. Enter WhatsApp phone if the field exists.

5. Choose your plan and continue with your path below.

8. Deal order and success

Purchase order: first shares B5↔S3, then debt B2↔S2 via Buy Now, then regular listing B1↔S1 via Buy Now. Auction is bidding practice only.
If payment hangs: do not tap again, take a screenshot, send listing/payment ID to the coordinator.
Test succeeds if: LOT-A…F are published; regular listing, debt, and share are bought via Buy Now/shares; buyer and seller amounts match; auction has bids (win not required); test-drive confirm+reject works; VIP unlocks private lot; no double charges; key events produced cabinet, email, and WhatsApp notifications.
