Talaïa Residences – New build homes in Costa Adeje, Tenerife

Talaïa Residences is an exclusive new residential development located on the coast of Adeje, Tenerife , with 232 homes distributed in six buildings designed to integrate harmoniously with the natural landscape and offer a unique living experience facing the Atlantic Ocean.

Premium Location: 
The project is situated on a 21,000 m2 plot with direct access to Ajabo beach and panoramic sea views, in Costa Adeje, one of the most sought-after areas in southern Tenerife for its climate, quality of life and top-level services.

Architecture and Concept: 
Talaïa's architecture is designed to engage with nature and take advantage of the natural slope of the land, offering optimal orientations, maximum ocean views, and a seamless connection between interior and exterior. The white facades reflect the Canarian light, while the wood and volcanic stone finishes add warmth and local character.

Types and Layout: 
The complex offers a variety of residential types with 1 to 3 bedrooms, all designed with bright spaces, spacious terraces, and high-quality materials. The homes optimize functionality and comfort, with integrated living rooms, en-suite bedrooms, and options for private gardens or pools in some types. All units include:

Fully equipped kitchen with Bosch brand appliances and a quartz countertop

Bathrooms equipped with Hansgrohe brand faucets

Individual ducted air conditioning system (heating/cooling)

Private parking space and storage room.

Individual aerothermal system for the production of domestic hot water

Common Areas and Services: 
Talaïa Residences features common areas designed for well-being and community , including:

Three outdoor pools, two for adults (one heated) and one for children.

Spa and wellness area with heated indoor pool, sauna, Turkish bath and gym.

Landscaped gardens with native species and solarium.

Digital parcel service

Lifestyle app (digital concierge)

Bicycle parking area.

Solar panels on the roof

Pre-installation in garage for electric vehicle charging

Commitment to Sustainability: The project incorporates 
energy efficiency and sustainability criteria , resulting in greater thermal and acoustic comfort, reduced utility consumption, and increased long-term property value. The energy rating will be B.
